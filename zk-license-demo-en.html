Technical demo

Share less personal information
when checking driving eligibility

Toppy is prototyping a way to check driving eligibility without sharing a name or address. This demo uses fictional licence records to check two conditions: eligibility to drive an ordinary car, and a recorded expiry date that covers the planned use date.

What the other party receives

The party making the check receives cryptographic proof that the conditions are met. The proof does not reveal the name, address, licence number or exact expiry date. The requested conditions and a record of whether the check has been completed are retained.

What Toppy implemented

Our code checks that the proof meets the requested conditions and prevents the same check from being reused. For example, changing the conditions cannot make a record that expires before the planned use date pass. The code and validation record are public.

A prototype using fictional records

The demo runs on one computer. It does not read real driving licences, verify a person's identity or check whether a licence has been suspended or cancelled.

The cryptographic operations use the open-source AnonCreds library. See the source and reproduction steps for implementation details, and the validation record for the checks performed.

mAI Economy

Can AI guide bank lending?

mAI Economy studies how lending guidance could change the flow of credit into production and asset purchases. We plan to compare AI proposals with simpler rules in a simulator.

Measurement code available / Simulator planned

The model Current work 日本語で読む

Where credit goes matters

Bank lending creates deposits as well as debt. [2] Our starting point is Richard A. Werner's Quantity Theory of Credit, which distinguishes credit for GDP transactions from credit for existing-asset transactions. [1]

Window guidance sets lending targets. Banks and borrowers may respond differently, or seek funding elsewhere. We want to model those responses, including the constraints on lending. [3] [4]

What the model needs

Consistent accounts

The planned model will follow lending, spending, repayment, and losses. It will distinguish production, asset purchases, and refinancing, leaving unknown uses unclassified.

Useful measures

Thermo-Credit tests ways to summarize credit composition and stress. Its experimental measures must be compared with ordinary balance-sheet measures.

Thermo-Credit methods and limits

What AI would do

AI would propose explanations and lending targets. We plan to compare these targets with fixed rules and simple rules that adjust to new data, including cases where the model's assumptions fail.

People set the objectives and permitted actions. The comparison should show who benefits and who bears the risk.

Measurement code

Thermo-Credit publishes dashboards, a paper, and calculation interfaces. The Japanese measure describes borrower composition, not each loan's final use. European and US measures are coarser.

The current scenario tool applies specified shocks. It does not model banks responding to guidance.

Next experiment

A lending simulator

Compare no guidance, fixed guidance, and adaptive guidance under the same shocks. Test what happens when banks do not comply or borrowers find other funding.

Model and evaluation plan

These are research proposals. Thermo-Credit's variables and AI guidance are our extensions, not results established by Werner. No safe-lending threshold or real-world policy benefit has been established.
